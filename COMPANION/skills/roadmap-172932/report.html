🚀 Career Accelerator: Create A 5-Day

Day 1: - LEARN: Research about the importance of environmental conservation in urban areas, focusing on London. Refer to resources like ZSL's London Wildlife Trust (https://www.wildlondon.org.uk/) or Thames21 (https://thames21.org.uk/). - READ: Read the latest news and updates from City of London Corporation's Volunteering Opportunities (https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/explore-volunteering-at-the-city-of-london-corporation/volunteering-opportunities). - APPLY: Sign up for a river clean-up event with ZSL's London Rivers Volunteer Programme (https://www.zsl.org/what-we-do/projects/volunteer-londons-rivers).

Day 2: - LEARN: Study about sustainable practices in urban environments, focusing on London's initiatives and strategies. Refer to resources like UCL Students' Union Sustainability Volunteering (https://studentsunionucl.org/volunteering/directory/category/sustainability). - READ: Read articles from Environmental Volunteering (https://environmentjob.co.uk/volunteering) to stay updated on environmental issues in London and potential volunteering opportunities. - APPLY: Join the TCV in London's project focused on connecting people with green spaces. Register through their website (https://www.tcv.org.uk/london/).

Day 3: - LEARN: Learn about the role of parks and green spaces in urban health and wellbeing, focusing on Parks for London initiatives. Refer to resources like Parks for London Volunteering (https://parksforlondon.org.uk/opportunities/volunteering/). - READ: Read about Parks for London's latest projects and news to stay informed about their work in the city. - APPLY: Sign up for a volunteering event within one of London's parks through Parks for London's volunteering opportunities.

Day 4: - LEARN: Study about wildlife conservation efforts in urban environments, focusing on London's unique species and habitats. Refer to resources like the City of London's Wildlife Conservation (https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/explore-the-city/wildlife). - READ: Read news articles and reports from organizations like the Royal Society for the Protection of Birds (RSPB) or London Wildlife Trust to stay updated on conservation efforts in London. - APPLY: Volunteer for a wildlife survey event with one of these organizations to contribute to their data collection efforts.

Day 5: - LEARN: Study about recycling and waste management practices in London, focusing on community initiatives and best practices. Refer to resources like the London Waste and Recycling Board (https://www.londonwaste.gov.uk/). - READ: Read news articles and reports from organizations focused on waste management in London to stay updated on best practices and potential volunteering opportunities. - APPLY: Sign up for a recycling event or a community clean-up focusing on waste management with an organization like Clean Up London (https://www.cleanuplondon.org/).
