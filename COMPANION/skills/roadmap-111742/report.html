Learning Roadmap

Skill: The Skills I Lack From My Skills Gap Report Landscaping

Day 1 (Landscaping Basics):

  • LEARN: Familiarize yourself with basic landscaping principles such as design, grading, drainage, and plant selection. You can start by watching videos or taking online courses from platforms like Coursera, Udemy, or LinkedIn Learning.
  • Example: "Introduction to Landscaping Design" by Udemy

Day 2 (Plant Identification):

  • READ: Invest time in learning about common plants used in landscaping, their care requirements, and how they grow. A good book for this is "The New Sunset Western Garden Book."
  • Example: "The New Sunset Western Garden Book" by Kathleen Norris Brenzel et al.
  • APPLY: Start identifying plants around your neighborhood or local parks to build your knowledge base.

Day 3 (Soil and Planting Techniques):

  • LEARN: Understand the importance of soil preparation, planting techniques, and spacing for successful landscaping. Watch online tutorials or read articles on the subject.
  • Example: "Landscape Planting Techniques" by Fine Gardening Online
  • APPLY: Practice proper planting techniques while working on a small garden project at home or assisting with a larger project if possible.

Day 4 (Hardscaping and Features):

  • READ: Learn about hardscaping elements such as patios, walkways, retaining walls, water features, and outdoor lighting. A book like "The Landscape Homeowner's Manual" can be helpful.
  • Example: "The Landscape Homeowner's Manual" by Alan W. Green and Susan M. VanDyke
  • APPLY: Observe hardscaping projects in your area to gain an understanding of different design styles and techniques.

Day 5 (Irrigation Systems):

  • LEARN: Study various types of irrigation systems, their installation, and maintenance. You can find online resources or take a course specifically focused on irrigation systems.
  • Example: "Landscape Irrigation" by the University of California Agriculture & Natural Resources
  • APPLY: Begin researching different irrigation system options for your own landscaping projects, considering factors like water conservation and budget.

Day 6 (Business and Client Management):

  • READ: Learn about running a successful landscaping business, including marketing, client management, invoicing, and estimating costs. A recommended book is "The Complete Guide to Starting and Operating a Successful Landscape Business."
  • Example: "The Complete Guide to Starting and Operating a Successful Landscape Business" by John O'Neill and Robert W. Laudati
  • APPLY: Start creating a business plan for your landscaping services, outlining your target market, pricing structure, marketing strategies, and operational procedures.

Day 7 (Networking and Practice):

  • LEARN: Connect with other professionals in the field to gain insights, advice, and potential job opportunities. Participate in local gardening clubs or attend industry events if possible.
  • Example: Join a relevant LinkedIn group or network with professionals on the platform
  • APPLY: Offer your services to friends or family members who need landscaping work done, allowing you to gain hands-on experience while building your portfolio. Continuously seek out opportunities to learn and improve your skills through practice.
