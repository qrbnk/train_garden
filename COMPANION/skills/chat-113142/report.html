I want to volunteer in London to learn about ESG, find volunteering

That's great! Volunteering is a wonderful way to learn and make a positive impact. In London, there are several organizations that focus on Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) issues. Here are a few suggestions: 1. **Corporate Citizenship**: A global consultancy focused on corporate responsibility and sustainability. They offer volunteering opportunities in various projects related to ESG. Website: https://www.accor.com/gb/latest-news/corporate-citizenship.shtml 2. **Business in the Community (BITC)**: A business-led charity that works with its members to create a sustainable Britain. They have various initiatives related to ESG. Website: https://www.bitc.org.uk/ 3. **The Prince's Trust**: While not strictly focused on ESG, The Prince's Trust offers volunteering opportunities and provides training, mentoring, and financial support to young people who are unemployed or struggling at school. Website: https://www.princes-trust.org.uk/get-involved/volunteer 4. **Greenpeace**: Greenpeace campaigns on a wide range of environmental issues, many of which align with ESG principles. Website: https://www.greenpeace.org.uk/get-involved/ 5. **Tearfund**: A Christian charity that works globally to help communities overcome poverty and injustice. They have projects related to sustainable development and environmental conservation. Website: https://www.tearfund.org/uk/get-involved/volunteer Remember, it's always a good idea to reach out directly to these organizations to learn more about their current volunteering opportunities that align with your interests in ESG. Good luck with your volunteering endeavors!
