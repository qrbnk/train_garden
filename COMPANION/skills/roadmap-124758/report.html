🚀 Career Accelerator: Create A 5-Day Esg

Day 1: Introduction to ESG

  1. LEARN:

    • Introduction to Sustainability - Coursera (Free Course by University of Illinois)

  2. READ:

    • What is ESG Investing? - Investopedia

  3. APPLY:

    • Research and compare ESG ratings of top companies in your industry. Analyze their performance and identify potential areas for improvement.

Day 2: Understanding ESG Performance Indicators

  1. LEARN:

    • ESG Fundamentals - edX (Free Course by University of Pennsylvania)

  2. READ:

    • Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Sustainability Reporting Standards

  3. APPLY:

    • Develop a list of key ESG performance indicators for your own organization or a hypothetical one. Identify data sources and methods for measuring these indicators.

Day 3: Integrating ESG into Strategy & Decision Making

  1. LEARN:

    • How to Implement an ESG Strategy with OKRs (2026 Guide)

  2. READ:

    • Integrating ESG into Corporate Strategy - BSR (Business for Social Responsibility)

  3. APPLY:

    • Create a mock ESG-aligned OKR framework for your organization or a hypothetical one. Present this to a small group and seek feedback.

Day 4: ESG Reporting & Communications

  1. LEARN:

    • Corporate Sustainability Reporting - Coursera (Free Course by University of Cambridge)

  2. READ:

    • CDP Global Reports - CDP (Carbon Disclosure Project)

  3. APPLY:

    • Draft a sample ESG report for your organization or a hypothetical one, focusing on key findings and recommendations.

Day 5: Stakeholder Engagement & Collaboration

  1. LEARN:

    • Stakeholder Engagement Strategies - Udemy (Free Course)

  2. READ:

    • Engaging Stakeholders on ESG Issues - McKinsey & Company

  3. APPLY:

    • Identify key stakeholders relevant to your organization's ESG performance. Develop a plan for engaging with these stakeholders, including proposed communication methods and expected outcomes. Consider volunteering time or resources to collaborate on ESG initiatives with local organizations or NGOs.
