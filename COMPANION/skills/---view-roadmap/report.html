🚀 Career Accelerator: 🗺️ View Roadmap

To help you gain skills in map viewing, here's a daily roadmap for three weeks:

Week 1:

  • LEARN: Start with online courses on GIS (Geographic Information System) and Cartography. Websites like Coursera, Udemy, or Khan Academy offer free and paid courses.

  • READ: Keep up with the latest news in Geospatial Technology by following industry websites such as Geoawesomeness (https://geoawesomeness.com/) and GIS Lounge (https://gislounge.com/).

  • APPLY: Volunteer for projects through OpenStreetMap (https://www.openstreetmap.org/), a collaborative project to create a free editable map of the world. They often have tasks related to map editing and data collection in London.

Week 2:

  • LEARN: Study advanced GIS techniques like spatial analysis, remote sensing, and spatial databases. Websites like ArcGIS (https://www.esri.com/arcgis-blog/) provide free tutorials and resources.

  • READ: Read local news articles related to urban planning and development in London. Websites like The Guardian (https://www.theguardian.com/uk) and even London-specific blogs can provide insights into the city's ongoing projects.

  • APPLY: Join a local community group or charity focusing on urban planning or environmental conservation. Websites like CPRE London (https://www.cprelondon.org.uk/) often have volunteering opportunities related to map creation, data analysis, and project management.

Week 3:

  • LEARN: Dive into specialty areas like transportation mapping, spatial epidemiology, or environmental impact assessments. Websites like Mapbox (https://docs.mapbox.com/) offer advanced tutorials on these topics.

  • READ: Follow research publications and reports related to London's urban development and sustainability. Websites like London Plus (https://londonplus.org/) publish data and research on volunteering, funding, and community development in the city.

  • APPLY: Apply for volunteer opportunities with organizations working on urban planning or sustainability projects in London. Reach Volunteering (https://reachvolunteering.org.uk/) is a great resource to find such opportunities.

Remember, it's essential to stay motivated and consistent in learning and applying your new skills. Good luck on your journey!
