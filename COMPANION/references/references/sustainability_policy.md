# GOLD STANDARD: Corporate Sustainability Policy
**Version:** 2026.1 (GRI & ISO 14001 Compliant)

## 1. Commitment Statement
[Company Name] commits to a 50% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 emissions by 2030, reaching Net Zero by 2045.

## 2. Quantitative Targets (The "Grader" Check)
- **Energy:** 100% renewable energy procurement for all sites by 2027.
- **Waste:** Zero-waste-to-landfill verification across 90% of operations.
- **Water:** 20% improvement in water use efficiency in high-stress regions.

## 3. Reporting Standards
All data must be collected in alignment with the Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) 302 (Energy) and 305 (Emissions) disclosures.

## 4. Governance
Monthly reviews by the Sustainability Committee with quarterly board-level oversight.
