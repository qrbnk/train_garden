30-Day Career Plan: 30-Day Esg Reporting Analyst

Here is Daryna's 30-day career acceleration plan to transition into an ESG reporting analyst role:

Day 1

  • LEARN: Complete a foundational course on ESG and sustainability, such as "ESG Fundamentals" by CFA Institute (2 hours)
  • READ: Article on "The importance of ESG in business" by Deloitte UK
  • APPLY: Update LinkedIn profile with relevant keywords and experience

Day 2

  • LEARN: Watch a video on "How to read and interpret an ESG report" by PwC UK (30 minutes)
  • READ: Latest news from the London Sustainability Exchange (LSE) website
  • APPLY: Reach out to 5 connections in the sustainability industry for informational interviews

Day 3

  • LEARN: Practice using a free ESG reporting template, such as the GRI Guidelines (2 hours)
