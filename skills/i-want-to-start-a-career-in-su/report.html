🚀 Career Accelerator: I Want To Start A Career In Sustainability

Welcome to our London-based Career Placement Office! I'm excited to help you develop skills in sustainability. Here's a tailored plan for you:

Day 1

  • LEARN: Start by studying the basics of sustainable development and environmental management. Consider taking online courses like:
    • Environmental Management (University of Edinburgh on Coursera)
    • Sustainable Development (University of Sussex on FutureLearn)
  • READ:
    • Read about the latest developments in London's sustainability scene, such as the City of London's Sustainability Plan 2025
    • Check out articles from industry leaders like Greenpeace UK or Friends of the Earth England
  • APPLY: Volunteer with the Trust for Conservation Volunteers (TCV) in London to gain hands-on experience in environmental conservation. Visit their website and contact them about upcoming opportunities.

Day 2

  • LEARN: Focus on building your skills in data analysis and interpretation, essential for a career in sustainability. Explore courses like:
    • Environmental Data Science (University of California, Berkeley on edX)
    • Sustainability and Climate Change (University of Oxford on Coursera)
  • READ:
    • Stay up-to-date with industry news from Environment Job's sustainability section
    • Research the work of London-based organizations, such as Thames Water or the Greater London Authority's Sustainable Development Team
  • APPLY: Join a local community garden in London, like the ones organized by the London Community Garden Network. This will give you hands-on experience with sustainable gardening practices.

Day 3

  • LEARN: Develop your understanding of policy and legislation related to sustainability. Consider courses like:
    • Environmental Policy (University of Cambridge on FutureLearn)
    • Sustainable Development Policy (London School of Economics on Coursera)
  • READ:
    • Read about the impact of climate change in London, including articles from The Guardian or The Telegraph
    • Explore the work of London-based organizations, such as the Mayor's Office for Policing and Crime's Sustainability Team
  • APPLY: Volunteer with a local organization focused on reducing waste and promoting sustainable living, like the Zero Waste Collective. This will give you experience in community outreach and education.

Remember to stay focused on your goals and keep learning! What would you like to do next?
