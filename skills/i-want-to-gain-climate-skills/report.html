🚀 Career Accelerator: I Want To Gain Climate Skills

Welcome to our career placement office! We're excited to help you develop skills in climate action. Here are your daily tasks:

Day 1

  • LEARN: Familiarize yourself with the key concepts of climate change, such as greenhouse gases, carbon footprint, and renewable energy sources.
  • READ: Check out the website of the UK's leading climate charity, Friends of the Earth (friendsoftheearth.uk), to stay updated on the latest news and campaigns in London.
  • APPLY: Sign up for a tree-planting event with the Woodland Trust (woodlandtrust.org.uk) at Hampstead Heath in North London.

Day 2

  • LEARN: Study the basics of sustainable development, including the UN's Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), and how they relate to climate action.
  • READ: Read about the work of the London-based organization, Climate Action Tracker (climateactiontracker.org), which monitors government climate policies and actions.
  • APPLY: Volunteer with the Thames River Trust (thamesrivertrust.org) for a clean-up event along the River Thames in central London.

Day 3

  • LEARN: Explore the field of climate policy and governance, including international agreements like the Paris Agreement.
  • READ: Follow the blog of the Centre for Climate Change Economics and Policy (granthamclimatechange.infra.manchester.ac.uk) at the University of Manchester.
  • APPLY: Join a sustainability-focused group, such as the Transition Town movement, in your local London area to participate in community-led climate initiatives.
